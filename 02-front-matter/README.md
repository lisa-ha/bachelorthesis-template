# Front Matter

This folder holds all content from the title of your thesis until the introduction.

* [Abstract](Abstract.tex) -- abstract of your thesis. FB18 and FB20 confirmed that Bachelor's and Master's theses only need one abstract in the language of the thesis (German thesis = German abstract, English thesis = English abstract). *For PhD theses*, you'll need to add a German abstract to publish the thesis at ULB.
* [Acknowledgments](Acknowledgments.tex) -- your acknowledgments (100% optional, it's absolutely okay to just it).
* [Contents](Contents.tex) -- table of content, list of figures, list of tables, etc.

Only relevant for Bachelor's or Master's theses:
* [Titlepage](Titlepage.tex) -- the title page of your thesis.
* [Titleback](Titleback.tex) -- second page with your thesis number, date of submission, names of your supervisors, etc.

Only relevant for PhD thesis:
* [TitlepagePhD](TitlepagePhD.tex) -- the title page of your thesis.
* [TitlebackPhD](TitlebackPhD.tex) -- second page with the date of submission, tuprints URN, etc.
* [Dedication](Dedication.tex) -- a short dedication (100% optional, it's absolutely okay to just it).
* [Publications](Publications.tex) (for cumulative PhD theses) -- your published papers and articles.
* [Collaborations](Collaborations.tex)  - mostly relevant for cumulative dissertations.
